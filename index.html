Smoking & Cessation: Doll R, Peto R, et al. Mortality in relation to smoking: 50 years' observations on male British doctors. BMJ (2004). Serves as the baseline pack-year cumulative risk model.

Bidi Toxicity: Prasad R, et al. Bidi smoking and lung cancer. Indian J Cancer. Accounts for the 1.5× to 2× higher tar and nicotine yield per gram compared to standard manufactured cigarettes.

Radon Gas: Darby S, et al. Radon in homes and risk of lung cancer: collaborative analysis of individual data from 13 European case-control studies. BMJ (2005).

Air Pollution (PM2.5): Loomis D, et al. The carcinogenicity of outdoor air pollution. Lancet Oncol (2013). IARC Monograph linking ambient fine particulate matter to adenocarcinoma.

Asbestos Synergy: Markowitz SB, et al. Asbestos, asbestosis, smoking, and lung cancer. Am J Respir Crit Care Med (2013). Demonstrates the massive (~50×) synergistic multiplicative risk of combined asbestos and smoking.
